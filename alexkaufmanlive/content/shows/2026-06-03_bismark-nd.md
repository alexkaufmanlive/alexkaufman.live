## The Lineup

**Headliner: Serena Thiel**
Serena is an Austin, TX–based standup comedian and a longtime friend of mine (I still occasionally call her Rena — the new stage name has not stuck with me yet). She's a two-time Kill Tony Netflix contestant and earned an invited spot at a Kill Tony Showcase at Madison Square Garden in 2025. Her comedy is could be described as dark and uplifting. Follow her on Instagram at [@shut.up.serena](https://instagram.com/shut.up.serena).

**Sampson & Alex Ward (LAUGH701)**
Sampson and Alex Ward are both members of LAUGH701, the standup group running comedy shows all across the North Dakota tri-state area. The duo has headlined sold-out rooms together, including at Dakota Stage Theater here in Bismarck, and they're bringing that same energy to The Jousting Lemur for this one.

**Alex Kaufman**
That's me. I am also on the lineup, traveling in from Montana to tell jokes and probably complain about the drive. More on me below.
