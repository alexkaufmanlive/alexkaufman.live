The [Laugh Out Loveland Comedy Festival](https://www.laughoutloveland.com) is a multi-day standup comedy festival takes place the first weekend of August in downtown Loveland, CO. Co-founded by Dan Bublitz Jr (Co-founder of Sno Jam Comedy Festival), Jeff Albright (Founder of LoCo Comedy), and Russ Bondi (Big Comedy Fan). This festival brings some of Colorado's best comedians, along with comedians from around the country, to downtown Loveland, America's Sweetheart City, for four days of laughs.
